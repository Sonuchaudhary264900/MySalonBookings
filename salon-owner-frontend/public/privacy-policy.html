Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2025

1. Introduction

Welcome to Smart Salon ("we", "our", or "us"). We operate a salon booking and management platform available to both salon owners and customers. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our application.

By using Smart Salon, you agree to the collection and use of information as described in this policy. If you do not agree, please discontinue use of the application.

2. Information We Collect

We collect the following categories of information:

  • Identity Information: Full name, gender
  • Contact Information: Phone number, email address
  • Authentication Data: Password (stored as a hashed value; never in plain text)
  • Location Data: Salon address and city (provided by salon owners during registration)
  • Booking Data: Appointment dates, times, services booked, and booking history
  • Device & Usage Data: IP address, browser type, pages visited (for analytics and security)

3. How We Use Phone Numbers

We collect your phone number solely for the purpose of verifying your identity via one-time password (OTP) during registration and login. Phone numbers are never sold, rented, or shared with third-party marketing services.

Your phone number is used to:

  • Send a one-time verification code (OTP) during account creation
  • Identify your account uniquely within our platform
  • Send booking confirmations and appointment reminders (if enabled)

4. Firebase & Google Services

We use Firebase Authentication (provided by Google LLC) to handle phone number verification via OTP. When you enter your phone number, it is transmitted to Firebase for the sole purpose of sending a verification SMS.

Firebase may collect certain technical data as part of this process. Please review Google's Privacy Policy at policies.google.com/privacy for details on how Google handles this data.

We also use:

  • Google Maps API — to resolve salon addresses into map coordinates
  • Google Cloud Platform — for hosting and infrastructure

5. Data Storage & Security

Your data is stored securely in our database hosted on cloud infrastructure. We implement the following security measures:

  • Passwords are hashed using bcrypt before storage
  • All API communication uses HTTPS/TLS encryption
  • Authentication tokens (JWT) are used for session management
  • Refresh tokens are stored and validated server-side
  • We do not store raw OTP codes — they are hashed immediately

6. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We may share data only in these limited cases:

  • Service Providers: Firebase (Google) for OTP verification, Cloudinary for image storage
  • Legal Requirements: If required by applicable law, court order, or government authority
  • Business Transfer: In connection with a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets

7. Data Retention

We retain your personal data for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide services. You may request deletion of your account and associated data at any time by contacting us at the email below. Booking records may be retained for legal or financial compliance purposes.

8. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access the personal information we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of your account and personal data
  • Withdraw consent for communications (e.g. SMS reminders)
  • Lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority

9. Children's Privacy

Smart Salon is not intended for children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will delete it promptly.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify users of significant changes by updating the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
