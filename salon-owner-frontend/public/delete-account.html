Delete your account

You can permanently delete your GlowLoox Partner business account and all associated data at any time. Here's how.

Option 1 — Delete in the app (instant)

  1. Open the GlowLoox Partner app and sign in
  2. Go to Profile or Settings
  3. Tap Delete Account
  4. Confirm — your business account and data are removed

Option 2 — Request deletion by email

If you can't access the app, email us from your registered address and we'll delete your account within 30 days.

Email glowloox@gmail.com

What gets deleted

  • Your owner profile (name, phone number, photo)
  • Your business listing, services and gallery
  • Your bookings, customers and reviews data

Some records may be retained briefly where required by law (e.g. transaction records), then permanently deleted.
